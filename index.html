About Tyler

A teacher and performer who understands the work behind the music.

Tyler Thompson has been a professional singer since age 9, with experience in recording studios and on theatre stages. Since 2017, he has taught music in schools and private lessons.

He music directs several local musicals each year and performs across musical theatre, opera, country, and gospel. His teaching is practical, encouraging, and centered on helping each student use their own voice with more confidence and control.
